# adeshgairola.com design context

Read this before any design work. It describes the site as it is now: a one-screen sketchbook after the grug and Arin apps. The v1 design (Geist, cobalt, white page, dark mode) is gone. Don't bring any of it back.

## The idea

A working notebook in public. Each section is one page of a sketchbook: a thin, shaky line drawing with plenty of air around it, and a few words in the bottom 30% of the screen. Anyone curious taps "… more" and the words panel grows to show the detail.

The site should feel drawn by a person, not assembled from components. It's lived-in rather than polished, and it stays professional. The drawings are objects (a desk, a flask, a clock), never characters or mascots.

## Who it's for

Everyone skims first. The glance lines get one look before a visitor decides to tap "… more" or leave.

1. **CISOs and security leaders** checking whether Adesh has done this at their scale. They need proof in the first lines: built AWS's ANZ AI security practice, co-wrote its threat model for AI apps, shipped the guardrail layer behind the first bank GenAI deployments under APRA CPS 234.
2. **Builders and security practitioners** arriving from LinkedIn, a talk or a search. They want a quick credibility read, then the writing and the open-source work.
3. **Conference organisers, podcast hosts and journalists.** They need the bio, a photo, recent writing and a way to book time, in under a minute.
4. **Possible design partners and advisors** for raxIT Labs. They need current focus, past scale and the calendar link.

Every section answers part of one question. Who is Adesh, what has he shipped, and where can I hear him? Answer it fast, with no marketing.

## Voice

Direct, opinionated, considered. Short sentences that state facts. Numbers, dates, named tools and named regulators beat adjectives. If a line could sit unchanged on any AI-security startup's page, cut it.

- Lowercase everywhere, except acronyms (AI, AWS, ANZ, CEO, APRA, GenAI, iOS) and brand and product names (raxIT Labs, raxIT AI, raxIT Studio, myCocoon).
- `src/lib/voice.ts` applies the same rule to text pulled in from feeds, raxitlabs.com and `background.ts`. Run any new pulled-in text through `voice()`.
- No em dashes, anywhere. Use a period.
- Security for AI is the main work. AI for security is the sideline. Don't frame it as 50/50.

## Structure

Eight sections, one component. Every route renders `Sketchbook.astro` and shows only its own section.

| Path | Bar label | Drawing |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | (wordmark) | desk |
| `/raxit` | raxIT | hexagon with a keyhole |
| `/labs` | labs | flask |
| `/work` | work | stack of layers |
| `/writing` | writing | open book |
| `/now` | now | clock |
| `/side` | cocoon | phone with a sprout |
| `/background` | about | ribbon, the career line |

The screen is a three-row grid.

1. **Header.** The wordmark `adesh.` on the left goes home. The scribbled shuffle dot sits on the right.
2. **Stage.** The drawing fills the middle.
3. **Panel.** Glance lines take at least the bottom 30%, with the section bar pinned at the very bottom. When "… more" opens, the panel grows to 64% and only the panel scrolls.

Every section has to fit a 390×844 viewport with no page scroll. On short landscape screens the glance lines drop to 19px, and the drawing steps aside while the detail is open.

## Colour

Two colours. A paper ground (`--bg`, default `#fcfbf8`) and one ink (`--ink`, default `#26231f`). `--soft` is ink mixed 72% into paper, used for secondary text. `--hair` is ink at 26% opacity, used for rules and underlines.

`--acc` is the day's accent. It picks one of six pastels by date, and it only ever fills one small closed shape in a drawing, like the coffee in the mug on the desk. It never colours text, links, buttons or rules.

The shuffle dot swaps paper and ink for another light pair. Half the time it steps to the next of four named pairs. Otherwise it makes a random pale ground with a dark ink of the same hue, and keeps it only if the pair clears 7:1 contrast. Each shuffle also moves the accent on by one. There is no dark mode, and the paper is always light.

The portrait on about is greyscale and multiplied onto the paper so it stays inside the two colours.

## Type

Shantell Sans Variable with `INFM` and `BNCE` set to 0, so it reads as calm handwriting. One face only.

- Glance lines: 21 to 28px, line-height 1.3, balanced wrapping.
- Pull-quote on the main screen: 19 to 22px.
- Detail and the bar: 17px. Year columns and notes: 15px, soft, tabular numbers.
- Wordmark and the contact oval: weight 600. Everything else is regular.

## Drawings

Each drawing is a set of SVG path strings on a 200×150 box in `src/scripts/doodles.ts`. Every subpath starts with a capital `M` and draws as its own stroke. Paths in `a` get the one accent fill.

The script samples each path to points and draws it on a 2D canvas with a pen that wobbles slowly along the line and trembles a little at each point. Drawings draw themselves on over about 1.1s when a section opens.

A new drawing should be a plain object made of a dozen or so strokes, with one small fill. Keep the ink thin and leave air around it. No shading, no hatching, no faces.

## Motion

- **Paper.** A WebGL sheet with grain, fibres and a faint vignette. It boils between three seeds every 140ms, like hand-drawn animation.
- **Drawings.** The draw-on plays when a section opens, then the drawing boils on the same clock as the paper.
- **Glance lines.** They type on with a person's pauses: longer after commas and full stops, longer again between lines. A transparent copy holds the space so nothing shifts while the text types. The caret fades out once the last line is done.
- **The about quote** fades in after the lines have typed.
- **The detail** rises 6px and fades in. A new section fades in over 260ms.
- **Shuffle.** The colours ease into the new pair over 500ms.

With reduced motion there is no boil, no draw-on and no typing. Everything shows at once.

## Details and controls

- **Lists.** Rows sit between dashed rules (3 on, 4 off) in `--hair`, with the year or date on the right in soft. No boxes, no cards, no shadows.
- **Links.** A hairline underline that darkens on hover. External links end in `↗`.
- **Book a call.** The one prominent control. It's text inside a hand-drawn oval on a canvas, drawn with the same pen. Without JS it falls back to a plain rounded border.
- **Section bar.** Soft text. The current section is ink with a 2px underline.
- **Focus.** A 2px dashed ink outline.
- **Touch targets.** At least 44px tall.
- **Separators.** `·` for inline visual separators, always `aria-hidden`. `:` inside data strings. `.` only for sentence breaks.

## Build rules that shape the design

- No client framework. Pages are static HTML plus one bundled script, `src/scripts/sketchbook.ts`.
- Without JS every route still works. It shows its own section with the detail open, and the shuffle dot is hidden.
- Section links are real URLs. The script swaps sections in place and keeps history in sync.
- Copy lives in `src/data/*.ts`. Writing and labs lists come from raxitlabs.com and Substack at build time, with hand-written fallbacks.

## Don't add

- Dark mode, or any dark paper.
- A second accent, or accent colour on text and controls.
- Cards, boxes, drop shadows, gradients or glass.
- Icon sets. If a glyph is needed, draw it with the pen or use a typographic one.
- Characters, mascots or cute faces in the drawings.
- A second typeface, bold headings or big display type.
- Scroll-driven reveals, custom cursors or page-transition effects beyond the cross-fade.
- Page scroll on any section, or more than about four glance lines.
- "Trusted by" logo bars, client logos or fear-led security imagery.
